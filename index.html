Kombo

Buying a used laptop in The Gambia means guessing whether the price is fair. Kombo is a marketplace where every listing gets a fair-price badge from a price model, and an advisor matches what you need and what you can spend to real listings, or tells you to wait.

I designed it before building it, then wrote the data model, price model and advisor as plain TypeScript with tests, and the React app on top. The honesty rules live in code: paid placement never reaches the advisor's picks, and a scam-priced listing is never recommended.
